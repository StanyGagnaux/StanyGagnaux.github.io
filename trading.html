Approach

My trading work sits alongside the fundamental equity portfolio but runs on a different logic entirely. Where the portfolio is built bottom up on company research, this side of the practice is built on intraday market structure across NQ1, Gold, and SPX: where volume actually trades, where trend is confirmed, and where large speculative accounts are positioned.

The stack combines intraday volume analysis, trend confirmation, and macro positioning reports into a single read on each market. None of these signals is used in isolation. The edge is in the overlap: a VWAP reaction that lines up with an EMA trend signal and a COT extreme carries far more weight than any single input on its own.

Signal stack
01
Key levels
Session and prior day high and low, overnight range, and round number levels for NQ1, Gold, and SPX as the base structure for the day.
02
VWAP
Volume weighted average price as an intraday anchor for entries, exits, and identifying where the market is actually transacting.
03
EMA
Exponential moving averages for trend confirmation and timing, filtering signals against the prevailing direction rather than trading against it.
04
COT reports
Commitment of Traders data to read managed money net long and net short positioning, flagging crowded trades and potential reversals.
05
Technical analysis
Price action, structure, and key levels layered on top of the quantitative inputs to time entries within the broader thesis.
06
News algorithm
An automated pipeline that scans news flow for market moving headlines and pushes structured alerts straight to Telegram.
News pipeline

The news component runs independently of the manual signal stack above. It monitors headline flow in real time, filters for relevance and market impact, and delivers formatted alerts to Telegram without any manual step in between.
